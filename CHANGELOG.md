# Changelog

The version is shown in the page footer. It follows [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH.

## 1.0.0 – 2026-11-22

First public release.

- Class mode with a guided setup, levels (high/middle/low) paired by neighbouring levels, near-the-board seats, rules, and rotation with seat history
- Event mode with long and round tables, a floor plan, guests and children, seating rules, and locked seats
- Automatic seating, print, CSV/JSON export, and share links
- Danish and English; dark and light mode
- No cookies, no tracking; data stays in the browser
